## Question
Who has to do this commitment, according to the transcript?
- reported_speech: true if the speaker is repeating what they or someone else said to a
  third party ("yo les dije, pásenme las notas"; "él nos dijo que…"). A request inside
  reported speech was for that third party, not for the people receiving this audio.
- owner:
  - "person": a person or party named in the transcript, e.g. "Jorge", "el desarrollador",
    "la Asociación de Desarrollo" → write it in who. Also when a third party has to meet a
    requirement ("el desarrollador tiene que abrir las calles").
  - "speaker": the person speaking will do it ("yo les mando", "se los daré").
  - "recipients": the speaker is asking the people receiving this audio to do it, now
    ("traigan los regalitos", "les pido que firmen").
  - "nobody": the transcript doesn't say.
- who: only for "person", in {language}; otherwise "".
